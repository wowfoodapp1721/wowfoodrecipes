Honey Sesame Chicken

Crispy skillet chicken tossed in glistening sweet honey-soy glaze with toasted sesame seeds.

Ingredients
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 1/3 cup raw honey
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 scallions chopped
Key Steps
  1. 1. Dice & coat chicken
  2. 2. Pan-fry golden crisp
  3. 3. Whisk & pour glaze
  4. 4. Garnish & serve hot
