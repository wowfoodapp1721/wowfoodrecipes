Honey Sesame Chicken

To freeze simply follow all directions except the cooking. Place in a gallon freezer bag. Be...

schedulePrep: 10 min Cook: 20 min
4 servings
4 boneless skinless chicken breasts
salt
pepper
1 cup honey
1/2 cup soy sauce
1/4 cup diced onions
2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
2 cloves minced garlic
timerTotal time: 30 minutes
4 Steps
1

Prepare Chicken & Sauce

Cut chicken breasts into bite-sized 1-inch cubes. Lightly season with salt and pepper. In a medium bowl, whisk together honey, soy sauce, and minced garlic until fully dissolved.

2

Sear in Skillet

Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add diced chicken pieces in a single layer and sear undisturbed for 3-4 minutes until golden browned on the edges.

3

Glaze & Simmer

Pour honey sesame glaze over the chicken. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer for 5-6 minutes until sauce bubbles and thickens into a glossy coat adhering to each piece.

4

Garnish & Plate

Remove from heat, generously toss with toasted sesame seeds and fresh scallions. Serve immediately over steamed jasmine rice or noodles.
