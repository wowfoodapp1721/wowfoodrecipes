Honey Sesame Chicken

Crispy chicken bites coated in glossy honey soy glaze

Ingredients
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • Toasted sesame
Key Steps
  1. Dice & coat chicken
  2. Pan-fry golden crisp
  3. Pour honey glaze
  4. Garnish & serve
