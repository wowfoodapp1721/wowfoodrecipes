Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Cold Fermentation & Proofing

COOKING NOW
hourglass_topPROOFING CLOCK24:00:00

Dissolve yeast in 200ml cold water. Mix with flour and salt, kneading until elastic. Bulk ferment in sealed glass for 24 hours at 18°C.

2

Hand-Stretch the Cornicione

Never use a rolling pin. Gently press air bubbles outward from center toward the outer rim using fingertips to form the airy outer crust.

3

Layer San Marzano Reduction

Crush whole canned San Marzano tomatoes with hand-torn basil and a pinch of salt. Ladle outward in spirals leaving a 2cm border bare.

4

Flash Bake in Woodfired Chamber

Slide onto pre-heated stone at 450°C (840°F). Bake for 90 seconds, rotating once at the 45-second mark for signature leopard charring.

5

Garnish & Infuse

Immediately top with hand-shredded fresh basil, a swirl of cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil, and serve scorching hot.
