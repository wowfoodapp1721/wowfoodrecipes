Honey Sesame Chicken

timer Total Cook Time: 30 mins
4 Easy Steps
1

Prep & Coat the Chicken

(10 mins)

Cut 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts into 1-inch bite-sized cubes. Season generously with salt and pepper, then toss the chicken cubes thoroughly in a bowl with cornstarch until every piece is lightly and evenly coated.

restaurant_menu Preparation
2

Pan-Fry to Golden Crisp

(10 mins)

Heat 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the coated chicken pieces in a single layer and fry for 8 to 10 minutes, turning occasionally, until the chicken crust turns crisp and beautifully golden brown.

skillet Medium-High Heat
3

Whisk & Add the Glaze

(3 mins)

In a separate small bowl, whisk together 1/3 cup honey, 1/4 cup soy sauce, 2 tablespoons ketchup, and 1 tablespoon sesame oil. Pour this sweet and savory sauce mixture directly into the skillet over the hot chicken.

soup_kitchen Simmer & Glaze
4

Toss, Garnish & Serve

(3 mins)

Toss everything continuously for 2 to 3 minutes over the heat until the glaze thickens perfectly and completely coats the chicken cubes. Garnish with a generous sprinkle of toasted sesame seeds and chopped green scallions. Serve hot and enjoy!

dinner_dining Ready to Serve
lightbulb
Chef's Pro Tip Do not overcrowd the skillet during Step 2. Frying in batches ensures maximum crispiness for that signature restaurant texture.
