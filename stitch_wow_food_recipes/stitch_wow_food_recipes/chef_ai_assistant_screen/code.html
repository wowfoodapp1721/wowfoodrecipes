restaurant Quick Lemon Herb Salmon

Perfect! This is high in protein, rich in Omega-3s, and ready in under 25 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 Salmon fillets (6oz each)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 1/2 Lemon (juiced) + slices
  • Fresh dill or parsley
  • Salt & black pepper

Steps

  1. Prep: Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Pat salmon dry.
  2. Season: Rub fillets with olive oil, minced garlic, salt, and pepper.
  3. Bake: Place in a baking dish, top with lemon slices. Bake for 12-15 mins until flaky.
  4. Finish: Drizzle with fresh lemon juice and herbs before serving.
