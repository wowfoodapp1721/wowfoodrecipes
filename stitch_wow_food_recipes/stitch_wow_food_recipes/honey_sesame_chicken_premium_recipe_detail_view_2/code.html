Step-by-Step Method

Audio Guide Ready
1

Velveting & Marinating

5 min

Coat chicken cubes evenly with cornstarch, light soy sauce, and sea salt. Let rest for 10 minutes to lock in juiciness and promote golden crispiness.

2

Crisp Pan-Searing

8 min

Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a wok or deep skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken pieces in a single layer until exterior turns bubbly and golden-brown.

3

Glaze & Honey Infusion

5 min

Lower flame to medium. Whisk raw honey, garlic, ginger, and sesame oil directly into the pan. Toss continuously until sticky amber bubbles coat every bite.

4

Toasted Garnish

2 min

Turn off heat. Shower generously with toasted sesame seeds and fresh scallion batons. Serve immediately over steamed jasmine rice.
