Primary Technique Step 1 of 5

Toss the 1-inch chicken cubes thoroughly in a bowl with cornstarch until every single piece is lightly and evenly coated. Shake off any excess coating before cooking.

Chef's Pro Tip: Ensure chicken pieces are patted dry with paper towels prior to tossing for a glassy, golden crisp crust.
